Case Study on Poor Digital communication

A college student once emailed their assignment to the wrong professor due to not double-checking the recipient’s address.
As a result, the professor did not receive the assignment on time, and the student lost marks for late submission. This situation shows how a small mistake in digital communication can have significant consequences.
The problem could have been avoided by carefully checking the recipient’s email before sending, using a clear subject line, and confirming the email was delivered.
Additionally, writing professionally and politely ensures that messages are understood correctly.
Simple habits like proofreading and reviewing messages before sending can prevent misunderstandings and help maintain a professional online presence. This example highlights the importance of responsible digital communication for students.
