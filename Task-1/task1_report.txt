I created my Digital Literacy Awareness infographic using Canva, as it is easy to use and provides a variety of templates and design elements.
My design focuses on important aspects of digital literacy such as understanding what digital literacy means, useful tools for students, safe internet practices, and maintaining a professional online presence.
I used simple icons and short bullet points to make the information clear and visually appealing.
The infographic highlights how students can use tools like Google Docs, GitHub, and Canva for academic and professional purposes. It also spreads awareness about online safety by including tips such as avoiding suspicious links and using strong passwords.
One interesting part of this task was designing the layout and choosing the right icons to match each topic. Initially, it was slightly difficult to organize all the information in a single page without making it look crowded, but Canva templates helped me manage the design effectively.
