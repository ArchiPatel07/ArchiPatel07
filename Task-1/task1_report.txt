Project Report – Digital Literacy Awareness Infographic
For this task, I used Canva to design my digital literacy awareness infographic. I chose Canva because it is easy to use and provides many templates, icons, and design elements that help in creating visually appealing content.
My infographic covers five main topics: what digital literacy is, useful digital tools for students, professional online presence,email etiquette and safe internet practices. In the first section, I explained digital literacy as the ability to use digital technology effectively and responsibly. In the second section, I included tools like Google Docs, online learning platforms, and communication apps that students commonly use. In the third section, I highlighted important safety practices such as using strong passwords, avoiding suspicious links, and protecting personal information and so on for forth and fifth part.
While creating the design, I found it interesting to combine visuals with short text to make the information easy to understand. However, one difficulty I faced was adjusting all the content into a single slide without making it look crowded. Overall, this task helped me improve both my design and digital awareness skills.
