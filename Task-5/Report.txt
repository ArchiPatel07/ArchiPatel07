Digital Literacy Course Tasks
In this project, I completed five different tasks related to digital literacy using various tools and platforms.
For Task 1, I created an infographic using Canva to explain digital literacy, useful tools, safe internet practices, professional online presence, and email etiquette.
For Task 2, I explored digital tools and understood how they can improve productivity and communication for students.
In Task 3, I worked on a coding problem using HackerRank, which helped me improve my logical thinking and problem-solving skills.
Task 4 involved writing a formal email, where I learned the importance of proper structure, clarity, and professional tone in communication.
For Task 5, I created a cybercrime awareness case study on cyberbullying, explaining its impact and ways to prevent it.
Through this project, I learned how to use digital tools effectively, stay safe online, and communicate professionally. The most interesting part was designing the infographic, while the most challenging part was solving the coding task correctly. Overall, this project enhanced my digital literacy skills and increased my awareness about responsible use of technology.
